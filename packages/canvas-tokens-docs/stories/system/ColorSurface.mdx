# Surface Colors

Surface tokens are **themed variables** for the backgrounds of containers and components, such as
cards, navigation, popovers, modals, and callouts. They sit on top of the
[page background](/docs/docs-system-tokens-color-background--docs) and adapt to light and dark
mode. Status and AI surfaces are not tenant themeable. For tenant-themeable surfaces, see
[System Brand colors](/docs/docs-system-tokens-color-system-brand-new--docs).

Pair each surface with a [foreground](/docs/docs-system-tokens-color-foreground--docs) token that
has enough contrast on it.

## Default

The standard container surfaces. `color.surface.default` is the base for cards and panels, while
`navigation`, `popover`, `modal`, and `raised` match specific kinds of containers. `transparent`
clears a background, `inverse` is for use on dark backgrounds, and `loading` is for skeleton loaders.

<SurfaceDefaultTokens />

## Alt

De-emphasized surfaces for secondary information that should read as separate from the default
surface. Use `strong` for a stronger distinction.

<SurfaceAltTokens />

## Status

Container backgrounds for informational, error, warning, and success messages, such as callouts,
banners, and badges. The `strong` variants are for smaller indicators that need more emphasis.

<SurfaceStatusTokens />

## Contrast

High contrast dark surfaces, such as tooltips. Content placed on them should use the matching
`color.fg.contrast` tokens.

<SurfaceContrastTokens />

## Overlay

Semi-transparent layers added on top of a surface to show interaction states, plus the scrim behind
modals. Use the `inverse` variants on dark backgrounds.

<SurfaceOverlayTokens />

## AI

Surfaces that mark content generated by AI, including hover and pressed states for interactive
containers.
