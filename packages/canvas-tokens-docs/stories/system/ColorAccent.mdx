# Accent Colors

Accent tokens are **themed variables** for solid colors that make small UI elements stand out or
convey meaning, such as status indicators, badges, and progress. They adapt to light and dark mode.
Status accents are not tenant themeable. For tenant-themeable accents, see
[System Brand colors](/docs/docs-system-tokens-color-system-brand-new--docs).

## AI and Contrast

`color.accent.ai` is a dark accent that marks AI-generated content. `color.accent.contrast` is a high
contrast accent for maximum visibility.
